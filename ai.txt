# World Cup - PropFirmDiscount.com

World Cup prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

19 deals from 11 prop firms, newest 2026-07-18.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
